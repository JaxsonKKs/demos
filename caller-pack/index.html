How it works

Your job is to find Alberta business owners who want to see a free sample of their own website. Jaxson does everything after that.

You
  • Call owners with no website
  • Get a yes to the free sample video
  • Book a callback time
  • Log every call
Jaxson
  • Builds every sample site
  • Sends the demo and the price
  • Joins every closing call
  • Takes payment and builds the real site

A win = an owner's name, email and a callback time, logged. Nobody pays on the first call.

The offer

A free sample of their own site first. If they like it: $500 to build ($250 to start, $250 at launch), then $50 a month and Jaxson handles every update. They own their domain and can cancel with 30 days' notice. The $500 is a founding price for the first 3 clients, then it goes to $750.

Why ours beat normal "vibe-coded" sites

  • Built around their trade. A barber's site looks like a shop window with an open/closed sign. A plumber's has a pressure gauge. A bakery never looks like a plumber.
  • Real info only. Their actual hours, address, Google rating and real reviews with names. No fake testimonials.
  • One useful live feature, like open or closed from their real hours, a delivery countdown, or a text-us-your-job form.
  • Made for phones first, with tap-to-call and directions at the bottom.
  • They see it before they pay anything.

The call

The only goal of the first call is a yes to the free sample video. No prices, no selling the website yet.

  1. Before you dial: check the Google listing. If it has a Website button, skip it. Find the owner's name in their review replies or on Facebook.
  2. Ask for the owner:
    "Hey, just trying to reach [owner's name]…"
  3. Opener:
    "Hey, it's [your name] with Mainfront, a web studio based in Stony Plain. I found you on Google and couldn't find a website for you. I'm not even sure it's something you need, but we can put together a quick version of what yours could look like. Can I text you a short video of it?"
  4. If yes, book the callback:
    "Great. Can we give you a call around this time tomorrow to see what you think? We'll have the price for you then."
  5. If they hesitate:
    "Totally fair. It's free to look, and if you don't like it, you just ignore the text."

On every yes, get all four: owner's name, best cell for the text, email, and a callback day and time. Read the email back to them.

Rules

  • Talk slowly and calmly. Sound like yourself, not a script.
  • Never say "I'm local" or "I'm in Alberta." Say you're with Mainfront in Stony Plain.
  • Never promise a price, a deadline or a feature. Say "Jaxson will go over that on the callback."
  • If an owner says "don't call again," log it and never call them again.

Objections

Keep every answer to one or two lines, then steer back to the free video. Log the owner's exact words every time.

"I'm busy."

"Totally, I'll be quick. Can I just text you a 1-minute video of what your site could look like? Watch it whenever."

"I don't need a website."

"Fair. Most of your customers probably find you on Google already. This just shows them your hours, reviews and a call button when they do. Want to see it? It's free."

"How much is it?"

"Depends on what you'd want, and the look is free either way. Jaxson goes over the price on the callback once you've seen it."

"Just send me an email."

"For sure. What's the best email? I'll send the video there too." Then book a callback.

"I get my customers by word of mouth."

"That's the best kind. When someone hears about you, they usually Google you first. This makes sure they find something good."

"Is this a scam?"

"Fair question. Nothing to pay and nothing to sign. You just watch a video of a sample. Here's our past work:" then send the portfolio link.

"I already have someone."

"No problem, thanks for your time." Log it as Has a website and move on.

Setup

  • Canadian number: get one before your first dial. Fongo World Edition gives a real Canadian number with unlimited calls to Canada for about $5 CAD, one time. Call Jaxson first so he can check what owners will see. If it shows "Spam Likely" or no Canadian number, switch apps.
  • When to call: Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 5 PM Alberta time. That's about 7 PM to 3 AM in Kenya. Salons, nail studios and groomers often pick up until 7 PM Alberta time. Skip weekends for trades.
  • Leads: Jaxson sends 25 at a time. Call only those. When you've logged all 25, ask for the next batch.

Pay and first week

You earn 50% of the build price on every client you bring in: $250 at $500, $375 at $750.

  1. A sale means the owner has paid the full build price. A yes on the phone isn't a sale yet.
  2. Whoever gets an owner interested keeps that client through to the sale: follow-up texts and booking the closing call.
  3. Jaxson joins every closing call to answer website questions. You ask for the sale.
  4. The $50/month care plan stays with Jaxson, because he does the monthly work.
  5. You're paid within 3 days of the full payment coming in. If the client backs out, nobody gets paid.
  6. Nothing is paid for posts, videos or calls that don't close.
