Smell burning?
  • Burning smell, smoke or sparks: get everyone out and call 911 first. Then call us.
  • Power line down: stay well back, keep others away, and call 911.
  • Breaker keeps tripping: leave it off and unplug things on that circuit. Don't hold it on.
  • Whole house out: check if your neighbours are out too. If they are, it's the utility, not your panel.
