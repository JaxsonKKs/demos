Questions

Common questions

Do I have to do anything?

Very little. I use what's already on your Google listing. You check the sample, tell me what to change, and send photos if you want new ones.

Is the sample really free?

Yes. You only pay if you want the site to go live.

Who owns the website?

You do. The domain is registered in your name, and your text, logo and photos stay yours.

What if I want to cancel?

Give 30 days' notice by text or email. I'll hand over your site files and help move your domain if you ask.

What counts as a small edit?

A change to text, hours, prices or photos on pages you already have. New pages or a redesign are quoted separately.
