Pipe froze? Do this first.

  1. Shut the water off at the main. It's usually near the meter in the basement.
  2. Open the tap the line feeds so melting ice has somewhere to go.
  3. Warm the line with a hair dryer. Never use a torch or open flame.
  4. If it split or you can't find it, call us. We'll walk you through it on the phone.
